Fill a PDF Online Free

FillPDF.app is a free, browser-based PDF filler with a full annotation toolkit: text, checkmarks, dates, signatures, highlights, freehand drawing, shapes, whiteout and image stamps. Nothing to install.

How to Fill a PDF Online

  1. Drop your PDF above, or click Choose PDF.
  2. Pick a tool: Text, Number, Email, Check, Date, Sign, Highlight, Draw, Box, Arrow, Whiteout or Image.
  3. Click on the page where content should go.
  4. Type with the built-in keyboard. Press Tab to jump to the next empty field.
  5. Click Download Filled PDF.

Fill PDF Without Adobe

You don't need Adobe Acrobat or Acrobat Reader. Every step — rendering, filling, signing, saving — happens in your browser using open-source JavaScript.

That means your file never leaves your device. No upload. No server copy.

Fill PDF on Your Phone

FillPDF.app is built mobile-first with its own in-browser keyboard, so typing works the same on every device.

  • Number pad and email layout built in
  • Arrow pad for precise positioning
  • Sign with your fingertip

Select, Move & Edit — Tap Once vs. Tap Twice

The Select tool is how you move, edit, resize or delete anything you've placed on the page. It uses two gestures:

  • Tap once — the item becomes selected (a blue dashed outline appears). While selected, drag it to move it, or use the arrow pad (◀ ▲ ▼ ▶) that appears in the options bar to nudge it pixel by pixel. Press Delete to remove it.
  • Tap twice (double-tap) — the text editor and keyboard reopen with that item's existing content, so you can edit it. This only applies to text, number and email boxes.

So the pattern is: tap once to grab it, tap twice to type in it. Nothing happens to text unless you intentionally tap it twice, which means you'll never accidentally overwrite a filled-in field while repositioning it.

Three Keyboards: Text, Number and Email

FillPDF.app shows you exactly the keys you need for the field you're filling — no switching between layers just to type an @ or a digit.

  • Text — a full QWERTY keyboard for names, addresses, and any general-purpose text.
  • Number — opens directly on a digits layout (1–9, 0) with -, /, :, ., , and + ready. Perfect for phone numbers, ZIP codes, IDs and amounts.
  • Email — a letter keyboard with @ and . permanently visible, so typing an email address is a single row of keys instead of constant layer switching.

Each keyboard has a Tab key that jumps to the next empty field on the page, so filling a multi-field form is just type → Tab → type → Tab.

Move with Arrow Buttons

Not every device drags cleanly — so every selected item also has a directional pad in the options bar: ◀ ▲ ▼ ▶. Tap an arrow to move the selected item 5 pixels in that direction.

  • Works for text, checkmarks, dates, signatures, highlights, shapes, arrows, freehand drawings and images.
  • Perfect for fine-tuning placement when a drag overshoots.
  • On desktop, you can also use the physical arrow keys on your keyboard, and hold Shift to jump 10 pixels at a time.

So if drag doesn't work on your device for whatever reason, the arrow pad always does — it's a guaranteed way to reposition anything you've placed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fill a PDF online?

Open FillPDF.app, choose or drop your PDF, then use the toolbar to add text, checkmarks, dates, highlights, shapes or a signature. Click Download Filled PDF when done.

Can I fill a PDF without Adobe Acrobat?

Yes. FillPDF.app runs entirely in your browser, so you don't need Adobe Acrobat or any other desktop software to fill out a PDF online free.

Can I fill a PDF on my phone?

Yes. The site works on Android, iPhone and tablets. It uses its own in-browser keyboard, so typing works the same everywhere.

Can I add a signature to a PDF?

Yes. Pick the Sign tool, draw your signature, then tap the page to place it. Move or delete it before downloading.

Can I fill a PDF that is not fillable?

Yes. Place text, checkmarks, dates, highlights, shapes, images and signatures anywhere on the page, regardless of whether the original PDF had form fields.

Is FillPDF.app free?

Yes. Free to use. No charge, no watermark, no account.

Does FillPDF.app require an account?

No. There is no signup or login.

Can I download my completed PDF?

Yes. Click Download Filled PDF and your document is saved to your device as your-file-filled.pdf.
